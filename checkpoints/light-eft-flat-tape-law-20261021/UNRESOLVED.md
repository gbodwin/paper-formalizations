# Remaining obligations and acceptance boundary

1. **Accept and validate this source.** The finite-vector bijection and ideal
   law pushforward are new unelaborated claims. They require sequential
   independent source review, then a separately reviewed bounded exact-source
   compilation/resolution/replay/declaration-audit plan using fresh qualifying
   inputs. No compiler or CI is authorized by this source checkpoint. The
   accepted gate at c1aaa0a00 remains unchanged.

2. **Realize the ideal product tape by a concrete generator.** The module defines
   a law; it does not generate responses. A concrete primitive/algorithm must
   realize bias 1/(2f) and the full joint product law across all N positions,
   retaining repeated candidate keys as separate calls. Specify its source of
   randomness, state threading, termination and exact bias guarantee. Marginal
   probabilities alone do not establish the joint law. For a fair-bit model,
   general denominators are not automatically representable with a fixed
   bounded number of fair bits: choose and prove an appropriate exact variable
   bit-count/termination guarantee, or explicitly quantify approximation error.
   No such generator, guarantee or error bound is supplied here.

3. **Charge enumeration, tape realization and response access.** The schedule
   and inverse equivalence use mathematical data/classical choice. N is a
   cardinality, not a step-count bound. Construct and charge occurrence/host/
   repetition/edge enumeration, endpoint encoding, primitive calls, response
   reads, allocations and conversion from a finite vector to any literal list
   representation. Prove their concrete order and exact-once visits. The new
   law does not pay for these operations or turn a function into an allocated
   serialized tape. Existing local 5U+5 serialization/filter bounds remain local.

4. **Bank and changing-state caller handoff.** Interpret the actual filtered rows
   as a literal bank with the required `hostInput` representation, head-wins
   repeated-key updates and outside-key emptiness; reach the canonical caller.
   The accepted representative bank rebuilds draws with `setEdges`. Equality
   of edge sets is not equality of differently ordered endpoint lists. Charge
   either the actual representation handoff or every representative conversion.

5. **Aggregate costs and whole-paper scope.** Compose enumeration, generation,
   serialization/filtering, bank construction and caller execution in one
   operational/cost proof, preserving graph, parameter, weight and supplied-
   forest hypotheses, input/output sizes and the stated arithmetic/bit model.
   The concrete generator's probability-realization obligation remains open,
   as do any finite-budget approximation and caller-level failure bounds needed
   for it. Whole-paper completion is not established by an ideal tape law.

The accepted supplied-response bridge, ideal joint event law and canonical
corollary remain accepted with their existing provenance qualifications. They
are not reopened, rerun or altered. If the new source is accepted, it closes
only the finite-vector flattening/reindexing and ideal-law transport step; it
does not assert that the supplied inputs were produced by a random generator.
