# Measured projections in adaptive edge rounds

`BinaryShortScan` visits the ordered prefix of a list up to and including the first successful callback. It uses binary additions to accumulate the earlier fees and preserves the result and fee. The gadget pullback checks original adjacency, scans the reached mask and vertex entries, and reads the dyadic label on the reached endpoint branch. The fractional-row projection and cut lifting also read the typed indexed entries. Per-round Lean statements preserve the earlier paid result and bound the new recorded projection work.

`PaidDirectEdgeWire.edgeWithRoundWork` records the reached projections in the actual original edge solver. It preserves the complete earlier output record and any unread tape. A failed round retains its reached fractional-row projection charge. Sampling stops on the earlier tape failure, so later rounds and samples incur zero work. `wire_round_work_polynomial` bounds the combined earlier and additional recorded work using source bit length plus numerical confidence. Width-sensitive accounting for the rest of gadget construction, orchestration, and the source-to-cut implementation remains open.
