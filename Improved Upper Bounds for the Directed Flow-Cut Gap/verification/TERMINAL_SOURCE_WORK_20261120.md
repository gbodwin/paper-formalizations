# Terminal readback on the original vertex and edge inputs

`PhysicalSourceReadback.runWithTerminalCharge` serializes the typed input, runs the cached physical fractional controller, and reads the returned plan through its measured terminal reader. Each reached lookup records binary address and metadata work. The controller work remains a separate field. The plan and dispatch fee agree with the earlier cached execution.

`PaidFramedVertexWire.vertexWithTerminalWork` and `PaidDirectEdgeWire.edgeWithTerminalWork` include this readback in the original solver paths. Their result projections preserve the cut or witness and the unread literal tape for either mode, including rejection and exhaustion. The edge solver runs the physical controller on the actual encoded line input and retains the earlier measured line, price and output work with the reached gadget rounds. Both paths carry the reached framing records. The polynomial bounds use the literal source length plus numerical confidence and cover their recorded work. Full structural bit time remains open.

The original unmeasured entry again uses the earlier cached evaluator. Lean proves that its complete plan and fee record equals the projection from the measured cached evaluator. The all-positive original vertex fixture remains in the hosted gate after its one-hour timeout at the binary vertex source checkpoint.

The 36-bit vertex fixture preserved obstruction `(0, 1)`, tape, dispatch charge and rounding charge. Its readback charge rose from 5,537,940 to 5,621,943.
