# Compute an edge factor from rational input

`RationalFourierMotzkin.solve` eliminates rational affine inequalities and reconstructs a rational assignment. It stores the projected coefficients and the reconstructed coordinates in arrays. `solve_sound` verifies the returned assignment. `solve_complete` applies to solutions over ordered fields, including the real prices in `HasEdgeRoundingFactor`.

`RationalInstanceEdgeFactor.counterprice_none_iff` checks the bound for every real nonnegative price. The system includes one inequality for each valid cut in the original graph. Every strict violation can be scaled so that the price of each valid cut exceeds the proposed bound by at least one. If the system returns a price, `counterprice_spec` verifies those inequalities. `least_valid` and `least_le_of_factor` show that the computed integer is the least valid one for the given instance. `least_le_original_factor` compares it to the paper's `efg(n,W)` at the original total weight.

`RationalDerivedEdgeFamily.family` and `draw` take the rational edge input directly. `family_spec` and `law_spec` prove validity and bound the empirical and ideal marginals by eight times the original-weight bound. `draw_used`, `draw_valid` and `draw_law` check the literal unread suffix, valid outputs and the bounded rejection law. The executable family uses the vertex-count scale when its light-mass test passes and the actual-arc scale otherwise. `ExactFlowCutGap.edge_uniform_at_original_arc_count` also gives the finite law at the original factor for real weights, with a size bound proportional to the number of arcs up to the displayed logarithm. The factor calculation and the cut selector are exhaustive. The unrestricted near-linear horizon in the number of vertices and an efficient exact-factor algorithm remain open.

`RationalDerivedEdgeFamilySmoke.lean` checks a directed triangle that requires factor two, a single arc whose factor is one, and the empty graph with factor zero. It verifies a normalized rational counterprice for the triangle, checks the exact unread suffix on successful and rejected literal draws, and exercises the five-arc fallback that returns 81 cuts instead of the earlier 163.
