# Both multicut approximation factors in one execution

The original vertex and edge wire entries run the mass mode and then the
size mode. The second execution receives the first unread suffix. Binary
arithmetic scores both returned cuts with the original costs and chooses
the cheaper cut, choosing the first on a tie.

`PhysicalPairedVertex.complete` and `PhysicalPairedEdge.complete` give
validity and the exact suffix on every accepted input with sufficient
literal bits. The corresponding fair-tape laws identify the output with
the composed finite draw tree. The tape bound is twice the individual
bound. Both price comparisons and successful returned counters, including
the parser, have polynomial envelopes in the supplied source length and
numerical confidence.

The `approximation_uniform` theorems prove the minimum of the two factors
in Corollary 6 with a constant fixed before the graph and weights. The
same probability bound applies to the cheaper of the two price thresholds,
by its marginal law. A zero comparator gives zero price throughout
the relevant mode's support. The result can be instantiated with an
attained fractional optimum.

The executable fixture exercises consuming two bits in sequence and
selecting the cheaper second candidate. Separate real calls through both
paired wire entries check a padded zero-objective vertex instance, a
padded edge self-demand witness and measured rejection of trailing data.
