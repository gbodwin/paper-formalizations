# Rational edge cuts on graphs with two positive stages

`RationalTwoLayerCertificate.assign_accepts_iff` proves that the executable rank search accepts exactly when there is a rank assignment in `{0, 1, 2}` that never decreases along actual arcs and strictly increases along each positive nonloop arc. Zero-weight cycles are allowed. Self-loops impose no strict increase because a simple path cannot traverse them. The search starts at zero and performs `2n` rounds. If a certificate exists, it dominates every iterate, and a strict round increases the total rank by at least one. The total is at most `2n`, so the search reaches a fixed point within those rounds.

`RationalTwoLayerCertificate.separates` proves that two positive arcs on one simple path must have tails of ranks zero and one in that order. This gives a two-way separation on every path even when there are many distinct positive sources and destinations. `RationalRankedEdgeOracle.priceCut_spec` uses that separation to meet the exact least integer instance factor for every nonnegative rational price vector. The separate entry `RationalTwoSideOracle.runEndpoint_spec` proves the same priced result whenever the smaller of the distinct positive nonloop tail and head counts is at most two.

`RationalThresholdRepresentatives.exists_representative` proves that sorted rational breakpoints and the midpoints of consecutive breakpoints preserve both comparison directions to each breakpoint. Equality is covered. The oracle inspects exactly `2m + 3` levels for `m` actual arcs, including the endpoints and duplicate levels when breakpoints coincide. It chooses the cheapest cut in this list. The existing empty-cut test is applied first. If the exact instance factor is zero, the returned cut is empty.

`RationalRankedEdgeOracle.family_spec` gives one cut at the original total weight `W`. For a nonzero factor it uses the half-threshold cut. Any edge it deletes has original length at least `1/2`. Every original threshold demand is cut, and the deletion marginal of edge `e` is at most `2 w(e) efg(n,W)`. The zero-factor case gives the empty cut. `RationalRankedEdgeWire.draw_spec` proves that the binary entry returns the whole input tape. A valid frame outside the rank condition has an inner `none`. A rejected frame, including a nonempty explicit-demand field or trailing input, has an outer `none`.

The fixtures cover both orientations of the endpoint rule, exact zero, and rejection above the endpoint cap. The ranked fixture has four distinct positive sources and destinations, eight positive cross-layer arcs and zero-weight cycles within each layer. Reversing the prices selects the other layer. The binary sampler returns the eight-arc cut with the entire tape and distinguishes an unsupported graph from rejected input.

The rank condition is an additional sufficient hypothesis for a short uniform family at the original factor. General exact-factor computation, the unrestricted dense-graph horizon at the original factor, and the complete addressed-machine bit-time theorem remain open.
