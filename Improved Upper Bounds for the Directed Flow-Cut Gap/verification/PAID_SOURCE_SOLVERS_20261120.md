# Recorded work for the original source solvers

`PaidFramedVertexWire.vertexWithCost` and `PaidDirectEdgeWire.edgeWithCost` return a pair containing an optional result and the combined recorded work. Their `vertex_value` and `edge_value` theorems preserve the earlier result on every source and tape. On success this includes the cut or infeasibility result, the unread literal tape, and all old phase charges. The `complete` theorems retain the sufficient-tape premise and give valid output and an exact dropped-prefix bound.

Both `wire_polynomial` theorems bound the recorded work by a polynomial in the source bit length plus the numerical confidence degree. The theorems cover malformed input, tape exhaustion, and success. Packed rounding preserves the work charged before the final guard. Its binary fuel tests and decrements have their own bounds. The vertex and edge entries also retain preprocessing, work from earlier rows and samples, and costs incurred by the reached selection steps. The bound uses the existing mixture of binary and structural word-operation charges. Width-sensitive accounting for the remaining allocation, indexing, and control operations, including the original natural-valued packed fuel formula, is open.

The fixed fixtures cover two-request tape exhaustion, fuel exhaustion, a successful packed singleton, and exhaustion after two completed samples in a test driver. The original source fixtures return an empty vertex cut and the single demanded arc of a 44-bit edge wire, preserving the literal suffixes in both cases. The edge fixture also checks charged rejection of a trailing source bit. An optional three-vertex packed stress probe reached its 180-second cap without a result. The universal tape-exhaustion theorem and the fixed small execution fixtures each have their own evidence.
