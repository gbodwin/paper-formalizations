# Joined recorded work on the original edge and vertex sources

`BinaryEdgeGadget.buildWithControl` uses the measured filter and indexed reads on the actual labels, retained edges, vertices, adjacency and prices. The earlier gadget, its node order and prior fee are preserved. The extra charge includes the reached child activity and binary additions of the earlier fees. Lean bounds the charge in the original graph dimension and the input price and weight widths.

`PaidDirectEdgeWire.edgeWithJoinedWork` carries framing records and boundary work from one literal parse and execution. Its reached rounds include their gadget construction, row projections and successful cut pullbacks. The same gadget supplies the rounding input and cut pullback. Tape failure retains the reached work and skips later rounds. The output record and unread tape agree with the earlier solver. The joined bound adds the paid work, boundary fields and modeled frame count and is polynomial in literal source length plus numerical confidence.

`PaidFramedVertexWire.vertexWithJoinedWork` carries the actual cached fractional controller work and modeled framing records from the same execution. Its full prior paid result is preserved. The cache count is polynomial in original source length. The combined bound adds the earlier fee, cache count and frame count. Width-sensitive terminal readback has its own scoped proof. The joined results include the previous charge model for other operations. Full source-to-cut bit time remains open.

The four-arc gadget fixture kept 14 vertices and its prior fee of 32,893, with 1,039,467 measured additional operations. The 44-bit edge fixture retained its cut and unread tape and reported 1,788,065,137 paid operations, 193,496 boundary operations and 3,801 modeled frame records. A trailing edge source incurred the prior parser charge and recorded the reached framing work. The empty vertex result and the 36-bit obstruction kept the unread tape and recorded 1,502,578 and 7,668,311 cache operations with their respective frame counts.
