# Unverified ideal-law rounding quality extension

EncodedRoundingQuality connects the already proved finite-cut expected-size estimate to the actual guarded repeated rounding program. It transfers threshold events through the duplicate-free vertex-list projection, applies finite Markov on cuts, and uses the actual first-on-ties selector's exact power law. Constants are fixed uniformly before the graph and threshold; the concrete threshold is the natural ceiling of twice the maximum of one and the established expected-size envelope.

The exact tape-marginal law remains explicit. This does not assert that bounded-fuel fair-bit draws have the ideal law. No executable body or charge is changed; a finite-output-record assumption is never made. Bounded-bit coupling, graph-provider composition and complete runtime substitution remain open.
