# Binary integer cutoff and epoch parameters

`BinaryEpochParameters.compute` executes both logarithms by binary division
and comparisons. It retains the exact restart, exponent, cap, and historical
word charge. `compute_value` identifies the complete parameter record.
`compute_paid` bounds the additional binary work.

`BinaryIntegerCutoff.run` executes all three regime comparisons on the
measured binary inputs and calls the packed integer controller in the hard
regime. Its projection is equal to the existing physical runner for every
literal tape and every cutoff. Both amplified vertex modes, the edge
reduction, and the calibrated adaptive family now use this implementation.
The extra counter is included in the original-source polynomial through the
proved chain-size and cutoff bounds.

The execution fixture checks both fallback masks, the unread tape and
historical word charge, and the predicates at a 129-bit virtual dimension.
The latter test evaluates the gate without materializing a dense graph.
