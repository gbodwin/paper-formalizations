Its statement covers early tape exhaustion. `PaidTotalRawGraphExecution.complete_trace` obtains a trace of bounded length from the existing sufficient-tape theorem and trims it to the represented original input. The extension and the instruction bound belong to the termination proof. The executable receives only the original state and runs to halt or the first unavailable request.
