## New substantive chain
- WeightedDisjointPathSplit cuts a genuine simple path at its first mass crossing.
  Prefix and suffix are vertex-disjoint, joined by an actual old edge, and their
  ordered support concatenation is exact.
- WeightedPathPartition uses actual suffix-length induction to partition the
  whole path into simple subpaths of effective mass in[L,2L+D). No vertex or
  short final remainder is discarded.
- WeightedPathBlockFamily turns these into a disjoint actual-support family.
  An original attachment port chooses a unique block. At most two blocks
  contain the original path endpoints, and the union of those with at mostN
  vertices has at most2N vertices.
- RootedPathComponentCover proves from actual walk semantics that a simple
  root path uses one residual component; any simple path uses at most two.
- RootedPathReserve strengthens the existing construction by retaining its
  actual maximizing path and the reserve's support inclusion. Thus its at-mostN
  reserved vertices lie in at most two genuine residual components.
- ResidualPathDonorCredit derives, rather than assumes, released donor credit
  cL/4 from an actual long path. Effective node weights are at least2, intrinsic
  credit covers c(d-1), and at least half of each credit remains after own costa.
  Disjoint actual component supports prohibit reusing donor credit.
- RootedDonorPayment pays endpoint sets with at most2N vertices per donor
  component. Untouched components use their actual long-path credit; the at
  most two reserve-touched components use4Na of root spare, in addition toNa

  2K<=Nb,2a<=cb,2Na<=cL/4,5Na+future<=spare.
- WeightedDonorAbsorption constructs the connected coarse graph from actual
  component ports, derives the radius from its same actual path, lifts confined
  paths to the original weighted host, and uses literal original unit connector
  identities for the paid certificate. No output radius, paid certificate or
  donor-payment conclusion is supplied as a premise.
