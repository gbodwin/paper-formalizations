# Lean change markers

Boxed L controls identify specific mathematical repairs or explicit finite proof conventions introduced while formalizing this paper. Each note states the original claim, the reading-edition or checked-proof change, and the reason. They do not label routine progress or certify whole-paper completion.

Hover or keyboard focus opens a note. Click/tap pins or closes it; Escape, Close note, and clicking outside dismiss it. The popover stays within the viewport and is scrollable on small screens. The marker exposes an accessible name, controlled note and expanded state.

The original reading text is preserved byte-for-byte when the added marker blocks, legend and asset links are removed. Mathematical repair versus finite convention is stated individually. The machine-readable `lean-changes.json` records the exact placements. Future source rebuilds must preserve or reapply these annotations.

Validation: DOM interaction tests at 320, 768 and 1440 pixels, unique IDs, ARIA relationships, keyboard/focus and repeated click/close behavior, viewport-clamp arithmetic, original-content comparison and unchanged existing interactive scripts. Rendered-browser checks are recorded separately by the publication owner.
